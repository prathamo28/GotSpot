## 💡 Success Tips

### Do These Things ✅

1. **Apply Widely**
   - Send 20+ applications
   - Don't wait for perfection
   - Parallel process, not sequential

2. **Network Constantly**
   - Attend events weekly
   - Connect on LinkedIn daily
   - Ask for introductions

3. **Show Traction**
   - Even 100 users is traction
   - Share demo widely
   - Collect testimonials
   - Document everything

4. **Iterate Quickly**
   - Update pitch based on feedback
   - Improve demo regularly
   - A/B test your messaging

5. **Be Persistent**
   - Most will say no
   - You only need one yes
   - Keep going!

### Avoid These Mistakes ❌

1. **Don't Apply Unprepared**
   - Bad pitch = burned bridge
   - Take time to prepare well

2. **Don't Only Target Top Tier**
   - Y Combinator is 1% acceptance
   - Apply to everything relevant

3. **Don't Ignore Feedback**
   - Rejections have lessons
   - Ask "What could I improve?"

4. **Don't Wait for Perfect Product**
   - MVP is enough
   - Traction > Perfection

5. **Don't Give Up Early**
   - Fundraising takes 6-12 months
   - Persistence wins
