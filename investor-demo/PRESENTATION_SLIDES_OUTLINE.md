## 🎨 Design Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: #1E3A8A (Deep Navy Blue)
- **Accent**: #3B82F6 (Bright Blue)
- **Success**: #10B981 (Green)
- **Text**: #0F172A (Dark)
- **Background**: #F8FAFC (Light)

### Fonts
- **Headings**: Montserrat Bold or Arial Bold
- **Body**: Open Sans or Calibri
- **Numbers**: Same as headings

### Layout Tips
- Keep 6-7 words per bullet max
- Use large numbers and icons
- More visuals, less text
- One message per slide

## 🎤 Presentation Tips

### During the Pitch

**Do's** ✅
- Make eye contact
- Speak clearly and slowly
- Show enthusiasm
- Use hand gestures
- Pause for effect
- Invite questions
- Be authentic

**Don'ts** ❌
- Read from slides
- Speak too fast
- Hide behind laptop
- Use jargon
- Go over time
- Look at floor
- Be defensive

### Handling Questions

**Preparation**:
- Anticipate 10-15 common questions
- Practice answers
- Have backup slides ready

**During Q&A**:
- Repeat the question
- Pause before answering
- Be honest if you don't know
- Keep answers concise
- Don't get defensive
