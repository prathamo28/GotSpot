## 💰 Equity Crowdfunding Platforms

### 1. **SeedInvest** 🇺🇸
- **Website**: https://www.seedinvest.com
- **Type**: Equity crowdfunding
- **Minimum**: $5,000 - $50,000
- **Fees**: 5-7% of funds raised
- **Best For**: Early-stage tech startups
- **Requirements**: 
  - Must be US-based or have US entity
  - Working product or strong traction
  - Professional pitch deck

**How to Apply**:
1. Create account and submit application
2. Prepare pitch deck and financials
3. Due diligence process (2-4 weeks)
4. Campaign launch (30-60 days)

---

### 2. **Crowdcube** 🇬🇧
- **Website**: https://www.crowdcube.com
- **Type**: Equity crowdfunding
- **Minimum**: £10,000+
- **Fees**: 5% + VAT
- **Best For**: European startups
- **Requirements**:
  - EU-based company
  - Clear business model
  - Financial projections

---

### 3. **Seedrs** 🇪🇺
- **Website**: https://www.seedrs.com
- **Type**: Equity crowdfunding
- **Minimum**: €5,000+
- **Fees**: 6% + nominee costs
- **Best For**: European tech startups
- **Requirements**:
  - EU company registration
  - Minimum viable product
  - Growth potential

---

### 4. **Republic** 🌍
- **Website**: https://republic.com
- **Type**: Equity crowdfunding
- **Minimum**: $50+
- **Fees**: 5-7%
- **Best For**: Early-stage startups globally
- **Requirements**:
  - Less stringent than SeedInvest
  - Open to international companies
  - Working prototype preferred

---

### 5. **StartEngine** 🇺🇸
- **Website**: https://www.startengine.com
- **Type**: Equity crowdfunding
- **Minimum**: $100+
- **Fees**: 7.5%
- **Best For**: Consumer-facing startups
- **Requirements**:
  - US-based preferred
  - Strong marketing/PR plan
  - Active social media

## 📝 Application Strategy

### Step 1: Prioritize Platforms

**High Priority for GotSpot**:
1. ✅ **Plug and Play** (Mobility program)
2. ✅ **EIT Urban Mobility** (EU grant)
3. ✅ **F6S** (Free access to opportunities)
4. ✅ **AngelList** (Angel investors)
5. ✅ **European Startup Prize for Mobility**
6. ✅ **PARP Poland** (Local grants)

**Medium Priority**:
- Republic (Equity crowdfunding)
- Seedrs (EU crowdfunding)
- Y Combinator (If aiming for Silicon Valley)
- Techstars (Multiple locations)

**Lower Priority** (Later stage):
- SeedInvest
- Crowdcube
- TechCrunch Disrupt

---

### Step 2: Prepare Your Materials

#### Required Documents

1. **Executive Summary** (1-2 pages)
   - Problem, solution, market, traction
   
2. **Pitch Deck** (10-15 slides)
   - Use INVESTOR_PRESENTATION.md as base
   - Convert to PowerPoint/Google Slides
   
3. **Financial Model** (Excel)
   - 3-year projections
   - Unit economics
   - Assumptions
   
4. **Demo Video** (2-3 minutes)
   - Product walkthrough
   - Problem/solution
   - Team introduction
   
5. **One-Pager** (1 page)
   - Quick overview for networking

6. **Cap Table**
   - Current ownership structure
   - ESOP allocation

### Step 4: Pitch Tips

#### For Applications

1. **Be Concise**: Most reviewers spend 3-5 minutes
2. **Show Traction**: Even small wins matter
3. **Clear Ask**: Exactly how much you need
4. **Use Data**: Numbers > Claims
5. **Video Quality**: Professional, not fancy

#### For Live Pitches

1. **3-Minute Rule**: Perfect your 3-min pitch
2. **Storytelling**: Start with the problem story
3. **Practice**: 50+ times minimum
4. **Anticipate Questions**: Prepare for tough ones
5. **Confidence**: Believe in your solution

---

### Step 5: Follow-Up Strategy

#### After Applying

1. **Week 1**: Send thank you email
2. **Week 2-3**: Check status politely
3. **Week 4+**: If no response, move on
4. **Rejection**: Ask for feedback
5. **Accepted**: Celebrate and prepare!
